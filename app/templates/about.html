名錄骨幹：臺灣物種名錄 TaiCOL

  • 有效學名、中文名、分類階層、特有性、外來種狀態、保育等級、紅皮書等級，全部來自臺灣物種名錄。
  • 透過 TaiCOL API 批次匯入{% if n_taxa %} {{ n_taxa }} 筆{% endif %}有效分類群{% if data_meta.fetched_at %}（資料版本 {{ data_meta.fetched_at[:10] }}）{% endif %}，物種頁的參考文獻也由 API 即時查詢。
  • TaiCOL 尚未提供上層分類的分類群（如 Actaea rubra）不自行猜測，統一放在「未歸類」。

外部連結：Wikidata 串接

  • 以 Wikidata 的 TaiCOL ID（P12218）找到對應項目，再取得 GBIF、iNaturalist、eBird、xeno-canto 的編號。
  • 沒有 TaiCOL ID 的項目，才以學名比對，且須階層、所屬科都相同、只有一個符合。
  • 每個連結都標示依據：TaiCOL ID、Wikidata 或學名比對。

照片：iNaturalist

  • 只使用 iNaturalist 上採用創用 CC 授權的照片，「保留所有權利」的照片一律不用。
  • 每張照片都標示拍攝者與授權。

物種描述：維基百科

  • 經由 Wikidata 找到中文（臺灣正體）或英文的維基百科條目，取用條目摘要，並註明 CC BY-SA 4.0 與原文連結。
  • 亞種若沒有自己的條目，會改用所屬物種的條目，並在頁面上註明。

維管束植物：WCVP 世界維管束植物名錄

  • 蕨類與種子植物的生活型、氣候帶、全球分布範圍與分布地圖，來自英國皇家植物園邱園的WCVP 世界維管束植物名錄（Govaerts et al. 2021, Scientific Data 8: 215），並連到 POWO 與 IPNI。
  • 以學名（不含命名者）將 TaiCOL 有效名對應到 WCVP；對應到 WCVP 的異名時，採用該異名的 WCVP 接受名，並在物種頁註明。有多個候選而無法判定的名稱不使用。
  • 分布地圖以世界植物分布地理區（WGSRPD 第三級）標示原生、引進、已滅絕與存疑的地區。

原則

  • 標明來源：每筆資料都可回溯到原始資料庫。
  • 不捏造：資料缺漏就照實呈現，不用名稱硬配。
  • 定期更新：重新匯入即可跟上 TaiCOL 的最新版本。

The checklist: Taiwan Catalogue of Life (TaiCOL)

  • Accepted names, Chinese names, classification, endemism, alien status, protection and Red List status all come from the Taiwan Catalogue of Life.
  • {% if n_taxa %}{{ n_taxa }} accepted{% else %}Accepted{% endif %} taxa are imported in bulk through the TaiCOL API{% if data_meta.fetched_at %} (data as of {{ data_meta.fetched_at[:10] }}){% endif %}; the literature on species pages is looked up live from the same API.
  • Taxa TaiCOL gives no higher classification yet (such as Actaea rubra) are not guessed into place: they are listed under Unplaced.

External links: through Wikidata

  • Each taxon's Wikidata item is found by its TaiCOL ID (P12218), which gives its GBIF, iNaturalist, eBird and xeno-canto IDs.
  • Only items without a TaiCOL ID are matched by scientific name, and only when the rank and family also agree and exactly one item fits.
  • Every link says how it was found: TaiCOL ID, Wikidata, or name match.

Photos: iNaturalist

  • Only Creative Commons–licensed photos from iNaturalist are used; "all rights reserved" photos never are.
  • Every photo is credited to its photographer, with its licence.

Descriptions: Wikipedia

  • The Chinese (Taiwan script) or English Wikipedia article is found through Wikidata; its summary is shown under CC BY-SA 4.0, with a link to the article.
  • A subspecies without an article of its own shows its species' article, and the page says so.

Vascular plants: World Checklist of Vascular Plants (WCVP)

  • Life form, climate, world range and the distribution map of ferns and seed plants come from the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew's World Checklist of Vascular Plants (Govaerts et al. 2021, Scientific Data 8: 215), with links to POWO and IPNI.
  • TaiCOL accepted names are matched to WCVP by name (without authors). A match to a WCVP synonym uses that synonym's WCVP accepted name, and the species page says so. Names with several candidates that cannot be told apart are left out.
  • The map shows native, introduced, extinct and doubtful records by WGSRPD level-3 region ("botanical country").

Principles

  • Sources shown: every piece of data traces back to its source database.
  • Nothing made up: gaps are shown as gaps, never filled by loose name matching.
  • Kept current: a re-import picks up TaiCOL's latest version.
